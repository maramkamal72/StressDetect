The model is a forest of 400 decision trees that vote on your level. Two people can both be {{ prediction.stress_level }}, but one set of answers may be a clear case (almost every tree agrees) while another sits between two levels (the trees are split). So this label can differ between assessments even when the level is the same. It describes how clearly your answers fit the level, not how accurate the model is.
The index (0 low, 100 high) shows where your answers sit inside your level's range. This is a screening aid, not a diagnosis.
